    Beds

    Top of the section first. Tap a bed to edit it. To move a bed, press ≡ on its left and drag it up or down, or press and hold the bed anywhere. ⧉ copies a bed on top of itself; + between beds inserts one.

      Extra symbols

      Palaeocurrents, structures or fossils at a given level.

        Samples

        Sample labels at a level, or over an interval, drawn in a Samples column.

          Data curves

          Measurements along the section (gamma ray, magnetic susceptibility, d13C, XRF…), each drawn in its own column right of the log. A CSV file with a level column and one column per curve, e.g. level;GR (API);MS (SI), adds all its curves at once.

            Orientations

            Strike and dip of bedding, cross-beds, faults, joints, cleavage, veins; trend and plunge of lineations and fold axes; palaeocurrent directions. Type them in, or lay the phone on the surface and measure them with its compass and clinometer. Drawn as a dip column (tadpoles) and a stereonet.
